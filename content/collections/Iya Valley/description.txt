Tucked away in Shikoku, the Iya Valley feels disconnected from the rest of Japan. This gallery covers a few days spent driving through mist-heavy peaks and along the Oboke Gorge.

The trip was a mix of single-lane mountain roads, swaying vine bridges, and quiet, vertical hamlets built right into the cliffs. It's a region rooted deeply in Yokai folklore, where the sheer isolation gives you space to slow down.

The final shots are from Nagoro—the "Scarecrow Village"—where hand-sewn figures outnumber the few remaining locals. It’s a surreal, quiet place, and a subtle reminder of how quickly these rural mountain towns are changing.
